## 3. AC-03 金额对平 — PASS（含口径说明）

```sql

FROM ta_order WHERE name LIKE 'ETM|%';
-- 86927 | 252,399,896.55 | 177,147,395.88
```

- Σamount_total = **252,399,896.55**，与 §10 基线分毫不差；
- Σamount_paid = 177,147,395.88，旧库 Σsum_pay_normal = 177,292,459.63，差 **145,063.75**。逐单对账（新旧各 86,927 行导出比对）：差异全部且仅来自 **160 张旧库已收>应收的超收单**（旧 paid > 旧 total），按规格 §4「越界回捞到期初调整」钳至 paid=total；其余 **86,767 单逐一精确相等**，无新增/放大付款的单据（increased=0）。此为规格明示口径，非数据缺陷（另见 OBS-04）；
- `amount_paid > amount_total + 0.01` 的订单 = **0** ✓。
- V7：Σta_bank_line.amount(ETM) = **195,342,327.43** = 旧库 Σpay_money(pay_money>0) 精确相等 ✓。

## 5. AC-05 发团计划 — PASS

```sql
WITH s AS (SELECT o.departure_id dep, count(*) c FROM ta_order o JOIN ta_order_line l ON l.order_id=o.id

SELECT count(*) FILTER (WHERE d.seats_used<>COALESCE(s.c,0)) mismatch,
```
